Who We Are

What is Mercy College of Nursing?

Mercy College of Nursing, Suddo Timergara is dedicated to producing highly trained, ethical and compassionate nursing professionals who serve communities across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and beyond. Our curriculum blends rigorous clinical training with hands-on hospital practice, modern skills labs and a strong foundation in patient care ethics.

From generic nursing diplomas to advanced BSN programs, we equip our students with the knowledge, confidence and clinical exposure needed to excel in hospitals, clinics and community health settings.

Quality Assurance

Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC)

Mercy College of Nursing operates a dedicated Quality Enhancement Cell responsible for monitoring and maintaining academic and clinical training standards across every program. The QEC reviews curriculum delivery, faculty performance, student feedback and clinical placement quality — ensuring the college stays aligned with regulatory and accreditation requirements set by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Nurses & Midwives Council and affiliated bodies.
